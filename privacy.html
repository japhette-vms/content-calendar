Content Calendar

A private web app where a social media manager plans posts for small-business clients, and clients review, approve, comment on and send photos or videos for those posts.

Open the Content Calendar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who this is for

Content Calendar is a private tool. Only people invited by the administrator (your social media manager) can sign in. It is not offered to the general public.

What information the app handles

  • Account details: your name and email address, used to sign you in.
  • Calendar content: posts, captions, hashtags, dates, approval decisions and comments you and your manager write.
  • Media you upload: photos and short videos you send for your posts.

How it is used

This information is used only to run the calendar: showing you your posts, recording approvals and comments, sending email notifications about those actions, and storing the photos and videos that go with your posts. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

Google Drive

When you upload a photo or video, the app saves a copy to a folder in the administrator's Google Drive so it can be used for your post. The app requests only the narrow drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files it creates itself. It cannot browse, read or change any other file in the Drive. Google user data is used only for this purpose and is never shared with third parties or used to train AI models.

Where data is stored

Calendar data and account details are stored with Supabase (a database and authentication provider). Uploaded media is held briefly in Supabase storage and then moved to Google Drive; the temporary copy is deleted. Notification emails are sent through an email delivery service.

Who can see your information

Each client can see only their own posts, comments and uploads. The administrator can see all clients' data. Internal notes written by the administrator are never shown to clients.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while you use the service. To have your account, posts or uploaded media removed, ask the administrator who invited you, and they will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
